I chose to focus on MOHELA's affect on student loan borrowers in the tri-state area.



New York is the leading source of Student Loan forgiveness complaints in the Tri-State area, according to a study done by Consumer Finance.

According to the study, New York citzens filed 271 compliants regarding student loan forgiveness between in the past three years, where New Jerseyans and Pennsylvanians did not surpass 200 complaints in the same period.

In those three states, the The Higher Education Loan Authority of the State of Missouri (MOHELA) was the most complained about, logging 212 complaints across the area in three years, almost 100 more than the compnay with the second-most.

MOHELA has been a cited several times for "studen loan mismanagement" according to Protect Borrowers. In January, the American Federation of Teacher amended a lawsuit against MOHELA originally filed in 2024, claiming that the company has "harmed sudent loan borrowers." AFT alleged that MOHELA "deliberatly ignored its responsiblities to borrowers" to grow its business in the lawsuit amendement.

In response to the original lawsuit, representatives from MOHELA removed the orignial lawsuit on the grounds that the "alleged violations" related to a contract with the U.S. Department of Education.
